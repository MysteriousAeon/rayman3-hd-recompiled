Host dependencies
-----------------
Python/Tkinter, glibc, libstdc++, libgcc, desktop libraries, Vulkan loader/ICD and
audio services come from the user's system. No compiler, complete SDK, NVIDIA
driver, Windows DLLs or Python runtime is redistributed in this Linux package.
